StarGrid 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

StarGrid（套件名稱 tw.stargrid，以下稱「本遊戲」）是由個人開發者製作的益智遊戲。本頁說明本遊戲會處理哪些資料、交給誰、以及你可以怎麼管理。

我們自己不蒐集個人資料

本遊戲沒有帳號系統，也沒有開發者自己的伺服器。遊戲進度（目前關卡、盤面）只存在你的手機裡，解除安裝本遊戲就會一併刪除。開發者看不到、也不會收到這些資料。

廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（橫幅、插頁、以及自願觀看換取提示的獎勵廣告）。AdMob 為了投放廣告、計算成效和防止詐欺，可能會蒐集：

  • 廣告 ID（Android Advertising ID）
  • 裝置資訊，例如機型、作業系統版本、語言
  • IP 位址與由此推估的大致位置
  • 與廣告的互動，例如曝光和點擊

這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理，詳見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊 與 Google 隱私權政策。

在歐洲經濟區、英國等需要同意的地區，本遊戲首次開啟時會用 Google 的同意表單詢問你是否同意個人化廣告。你之後可以在主畫面的「廣告隱私設定」隨時更改。

付費去除廣告（Google Play 帳款服務）

「去除廣告」是透過 Google Play 購買的。付款資料（信用卡等）由 Google Play 處理，開發者拿不到。本遊戲只會向 Google Play 查詢「這個 Google 帳號是否已經買過去除廣告」，用來決定要不要顯示廣告。

你可以怎麼管理

  • 更改廣告同意：遊戲主畫面「廣告隱私設定」（只在需要同意的地區出現）
  • 重設或刪除廣告 ID、停用個人化廣告：手機「設定 → Google → 廣告」
  • 刪除遊戲進度：解除安裝本遊戲，或在手機設定裡清除本遊戲的儲存空間
  • 完全不看廣告：購買「去除廣告」

兒童

本遊戲不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

本政策如有修改，會更新本頁並修改上方的生效日期。

聯絡方式

對本政策有任何問題，請寄信到 testsupport.tw@gmail.com。

StarGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

StarGrid (package name tw.stargrid, "the game") is a puzzle game made by an individual developer. This page explains what data the game handles, who receives it, and how you can control it.

We do not collect personal data ourselves

The game has no accounts and no developer-operated servers. Your progress (current level and board) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game. The developer never sees or receives it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads that unlock a hint). To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • Your Advertising ID
  • Device information such as model, OS version and language
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks

Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In regions that require consent (such as the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form on first launch. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy settings" button on the home screen.

Remove ads purchase (Google Play Billing)

"Remove ads" is purchased through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are never available to the developer. The game only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns "remove ads", to decide whether to show ads.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent: "Ad privacy settings" on the home screen (shown only where consent is required)
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads: device Settings → Google → Ads
  • Delete your progress: uninstall the game or clear its storage in device settings
  • No ads at all: purchase "Remove ads"

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above.
